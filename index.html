About Us

At CMR University, our student community is driven by a shared passion for creativity, leadership, and holistic excellence. Powered by the Student Council and mentored by the Office of Student Affairs, we bring together diverse schools, clubs, and associations across the Lakeside Campus.

Beyond academics, we create platforms where innovation meets expression, uniting artists, thinkers, and organizers to execute large-scale experiences. Every initiative reflects our commitment to teamwork, integrity, and turning bold student visions into reality.

About Ranvita

Hosted at the CMR University Lakeside Campus, Ranvita 2027 unites talent from Bengaluru, across India, and international contingents. The fest features performing arts, coding, design, gaming, debates, and business strategy.

Beyond the main arena, Pre-Ranvita flash-mobs, social initiatives like Ride for a Cause, and high-voltage Pro-Nights build toward the ultimate battle for the Overall Champions trophy.
